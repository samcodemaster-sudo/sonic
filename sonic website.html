"If you are a designer or work with graphic design, you have likely encountered texts known as ‘Lorem Ipsum.’ Lorem Ipsum is placeholder text—meaning it is nonsensical—used for testing fonts or filling space in graphic design and the printing industry. Web and graphic designers use this text to fill pages and demonstrate the overall look of a design. When designing a website, designers often face the problem that the actual content is not yet prepared. As a result, the layout does not give the client a correct perception. If a designer were to search for relevant texts, their focus would shift away from the core work, which is time-consuming. Additionally, designers want to get feedback on the design itself and do not want others to focus on the text content. Since Lorem Ipsum closely resembles real text, designers typically use it to show the client or employer how the designed template will look once the text is inserted, and how fonts and sizes are handled. Another point regarding the Lorem Ipsum placeholder text is that some web designers and graphic artists forget to remove or replace the Lorem Ipsum text from various parts of the site after creating the template and intended content. For this reason, we often encounter websites where Lorem Ipsum text still exists in the corners of pages and has not been deleted, which indicates negligence on the part of the designers."
